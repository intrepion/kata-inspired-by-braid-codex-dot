# The Hours We Keep

## Language

**Keeper**: Iona, the traveler who carries the observatory's lantern.

**Memory**: An amber fragment that remains recovered when time is reversed.

**Vault**: A chamber too deep to escape by jumping; the keeper returns by reversing time.

**Bell**: An amber mechanism whose awakened state persists across reversal.

**Reversal**: Returning the keeper and the blue world to an earlier moment.

**Branch**: A new path taken after a reversal, replacing the abandoned future.

**Hour**: One restored room in the observatory.
