# The Hours We Keep

Decisions delegated by the user; no interactive interview took place. Original character: Iona, a keeper carrying a lantern through a submerged astronomical garden. Four rooms restore four hours, with an authored ending. Original procedural Canvas art and synthesized Web Audio; no external assets or paid services.

A 60 Hz deterministic platforming world. Walking, jumping (buffer and coyote time), hazards, death freeze, hold-to-rewind. Ordinary objects/player/world clock rewind. Amber memories and the final bell latch persist while rewinding; restart clears room memories. Releasing rewind branches: abandoned future snapshots are discarded. Thirty seconds bounded history. Pause, focus loss, menus and transitions clear inputs. Save/reload preserves room, memories, position and usable rewind history; malformed saves fall back safely.

Rooms: 1 teach retrieval from a deep vault followed by rewind; 2 retrieve two separate memories on separate branches; 3 obtain a memory then a distant bell, rewinding after each to unlock a bridge; 4 combine two memories, a bell and a moving pendulum hazard. Vault depth exceeds jump reach, exits require all objectives, boundaries prevent walking around gates. Completion advances only on an explicit button, ending permits replay.

Test seams chosen under delegated authority: public Engine tick/rewind/restart/serialize/restore; geometry and progression requirements. Test real physics paths through every room and failures when using ordinary movement. Browser play is independent evidence and must be described separately.
